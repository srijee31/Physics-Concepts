# 41. Wave Motion

## 🧠 Definition
Wave motion is the **propagation of disturbances or energy through a medium or space** without the actual movement of particles from one place to another.

---

## 🎯 Types of Waves

### 1. Transverse Waves
- Particles oscillate **perpendicular** to the direction of wave propagation  
- Examples: Light waves, water waves  

### 2. Longitudinal Waves
- Particles oscillate **parallel** to the direction of wave propagation  
- Examples: Sound waves  

---

## 🧮 Wave Parameters

- **Wavelength (λ)** → Distance between two consecutive crests or compressions  
- **Frequency (f)** → Number of waves per second  
- **Time Period (T)** → Time for one complete wave  
- **Amplitude (A)** → Maximum displacement of particles  

---

## 🔄 Wave Equation

v = fλ

Where:
- v = Wave velocity  
- f = Frequency  
- λ = Wavelength  

---

## 📏 SI Units

- Wavelength (λ) → meters (m)  
- Frequency (f) → Hertz (Hz)  
- Velocity (v) → m/s  

---

## 🎯 Characteristics of Wave Motion

- Transfers **energy, not matter**  
- Particles vibrate around their **mean position**  
- Wave speed depends on the **medium**  

---

## 🔥 Examples

- Sound waves in air  
- Water waves in oceans  
- Light waves in space  

---

## ⚡ Key Points

- Wave motion carries energy  
- Two main types: transverse & longitudinal  
- Equation: v = fλ  
- No net movement of particles
