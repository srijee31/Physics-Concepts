# 38. Time Period and Frequency

## 🧠 Definition

### Time Period (T)
Time period is the **time taken to complete one full oscillation or cycle**.

### Frequency (f)
Frequency is the **number of oscillations completed per second**.

---

## 🧮 Formula

T = 1 / f  

f = 1 / T  

Where:
- T = Time period (in seconds)  
- f = Frequency (in Hertz, Hz)  

---

## 📏 SI Units

- Time Period → seconds (s)  
- Frequency → Hertz (Hz)  

---

## 🔄 Relation with Angular Frequency

ω = 2πf  

Where:
- ω = Angular frequency  
- f = Frequency  

---

## 🎯 Understanding the Concept

- If oscillation is **fast** → frequency is high → time period is low  
- If oscillation is **slow** → frequency is low → time period is high  

👉 They are **inversely proportional**

---

## 🔥 Examples

- A pendulum completing one swing  
- Vibrations of a spring  
- Waves (sound, light, water)  

---

## ⚡ Key Points

- Time period = time for one cycle  
- Frequency = cycles per second  
- Relation: T = 1/f  
- Unit of frequency is Hz  
- Used in waves, SHM, and oscillations
