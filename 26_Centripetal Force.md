# 26. Centripetal Force

## 📌 What is Centripetal Force?

Centripetal Force is the force that acts **towards the center** of a circular path.

👉 It keeps an object moving in a circle  
👉 Without it, the object will move in a straight line  

---

## 🧠 Simple Understanding

- “Centripetal” = center-seeking  
- Force always acts **inward (towards center)**  

---

## 📐 Formula

F = m × v² / r

Where:
- F = centripetal force  
- m = mass (kg)  
- v = velocity (m/s)  
- r = radius (m)

---

## 🔥 Real-Life Examples

- 🪨 Stone tied to a string and rotated  
- 🚗 Car taking a turn  
- 🌍 Planets revolving around the sun (gravity acts as centripetal force)

---

## ⚡ Key Points

- Always acts towards the center  
- Required for circular motion  
- Greater speed → Greater force  
- Smaller radius → Greater force  

---

## ❗ What if Centripetal Force is Removed?

👉 Object will move in a **straight line (tangent to the circle)**

---

## 🧠 Example Problem

A 2 kg object moves in a circle of radius 5 m with velocity 10 m/s.

F = (2 × 10²) / 5  
F = (2 × 100) / 5  
F = 200 / 5  
F = 40 N  

---

## 🧠 Summary

- Centripetal force keeps object in circular motion  
- Formula: F = m v² / r  
- Direction: Always towards center  
- Needed to change direction continuously  

---

## ✅ One-Line Definition

👉 Centripetal force is the force that acts towards the center of a circular path and keeps an object moving in that path.
