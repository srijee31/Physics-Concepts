# 40. Damping and Resonance

## 🧠 Damping

### 📌 Definition
Damping is the **gradual reduction in amplitude of oscillations** due to external resistive forces like friction or air resistance.

---

## 🎯 Types of Damping

1. **Underdamped**
   - Oscillations continue with gradually decreasing amplitude  

2. **Critically Damped**
   - System returns to equilibrium quickly **without oscillating**  

3. **Overdamped**
   - System returns slowly to equilibrium without oscillating  

---

## 🔄 Effect of Damping

- Energy of the system is gradually **lost as heat**  
- Amplitude decreases over time  
- Motion eventually stops  

---

## 🔥 Examples of Damping

- Shock absorbers in vehicles  
- A swinging pendulum coming to rest  
- Vibrations in mechanical systems  

---

## 🧠 Resonance

### 📌 Definition
Resonance occurs when a system is driven by an external force at a frequency equal to its **natural frequency**, resulting in **maximum amplitude**.

---

## 🧮 Condition for Resonance

f = f₀

Where:
- f = Driving frequency  
- f₀ = Natural frequency  

---

## 🎯 Characteristics of Resonance

- Amplitude becomes **maximum**  
- Energy transfer is most efficient  
- Can cause **large vibrations**  

---

## 🔥 Examples of Resonance

- A swing pushed at the right time  
- Breaking of a glass by sound  
- Musical instruments producing sound  

---

## ⚡ Key Points

- Damping reduces amplitude over time  
- Resonance increases amplitude at specific frequency  
- Both are important in real-life systems  
- Excessive resonance can be dangerous
