# 43. Wavelength, Frequency, and Wave Speed

## 🧠 Definition

Wave motion is described using important quantities such as:
- Wavelength (λ)
- Frequency (f)
- Wave Speed (v)

These quantities help explain how waves travel.

---

## 🎯 1. Wavelength (λ)

### 📌 Definition
Wavelength is the **distance between two consecutive points in the same phase** of a wave.

Examples:
- Crest to crest  
- Trough to trough  
- Compression to compression  

### 📏 SI Unit

- meter (m)

---

## 🎯 2. Frequency (f)

### 📌 Definition
Frequency is the **number of complete waves produced per second**.

### 📏 SI Unit

- Hertz (Hz)

1 Hz = 1 wave per second

---

## 🎯 3. Wave Speed (v)

### 📌 Definition
Wave speed is the **distance travelled by the wave per second**.

### 📏 SI Unit

- meter per second (m/s)

---

## 🧮 Wave Equation

v = fλ

Where:
- v = Wave speed  
- f = Frequency  
- λ = Wavelength  

---

## 🔄 Important Relations

- If frequency increases, wavelength decreases (for same speed)  
- If wavelength increases, frequency decreases  
- Wave speed depends on the medium  

---

## 🔥 Examples

- Sound waves in air  
- Light waves in space  
- Water ripples  

---

## ⚡ Key Points

- Wavelength = length of one wave  
- Frequency = waves per second  
- Speed = distance travelled per second  
- Formula: v = fλ
