# 18. Potential Energy

## 📌 What is Potential Energy?
Potential Energy (PE) is the **stored energy** in an object due to its **position or condition**.

- If an object is placed at a height, it has potential energy.
- Even if the object is not moving, it can still possess energy.

👉 Example: A book kept on a shelf has potential energy.

---

## 📐 Formula of Potential Energy

PE = m × g × h

Where:
- m = mass (in kg)
- g = acceleration due to gravity (9.8 m/s²)
- h = height (in meters)

---

## 🔥 Real-Life Examples

- 📦 Book kept on a shelf  
- 💧 Water stored in a dam  
- 🎢 Roller coaster at the highest point  

👉 These objects have energy because they can fall and do work.

---

## ⚡ Key Points

- Higher the height → Greater the potential energy  
- Greater the mass → Greater the potential energy  
- If height = 0 → Potential energy = 0  

---

## 🔁 Conversion of Energy

Potential Energy can convert into Kinetic Energy.

Example:
- A ball at the top → Maximum Potential Energy  
- When it falls → Potential Energy decreases and Kinetic Energy increases  

---

## 🧠 Example Problem

A 2 kg object is placed at a height of 5 m.

PE = 2 × 9.8 × 5  
PE = 98 Joules

---

## 🧠 Summary

- Potential Energy = Energy stored due to position  
- Formula: PE = m × g × h  
- Measured in Joules (J)  
- Depends on mass, gravity, and height  

---

## ✅ One-Line Definition

👉 Potential Energy is the energy stored in an object due to its position.
