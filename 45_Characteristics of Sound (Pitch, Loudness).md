# 45. Characteristics of Sound (Pitch, Loudness)

Sound has different characteristics that help us identify and compare one sound with another. Two major characteristics are **Pitch** and **Loudness**.

---

## 1. Pitch

### Definition
Pitch is the characteristic of sound that tells us whether a sound is **high** or **low**.

It depends mainly on the **frequency** of the sound wave.

- **High frequency** → High pitch  
- **Low frequency** → Low pitch  

### Examples
- Whistle → High pitch  
- Drum → Low pitch  
- Female voice usually has higher pitch than male voice.

### Unit
Pitch itself has no unit, but it is related to frequency measured in **Hertz (Hz)**.

### Formula Relation

\[
\text{Pitch} \propto \text{Frequency}
\]

### Real-Life Example
When a guitar string is tightened, it vibrates faster and produces a higher pitch sound.

---

## 2. Loudness

### Definition
Loudness is the characteristic of sound that tells us whether a sound is **loud** or **soft**.

It depends on the **amplitude** of the sound wave.

- Greater amplitude → Louder sound  
- Smaller amplitude → Softer sound  

### Examples
- Shouting → Loud sound  
- Whispering → Soft sound  

### Unit
Loudness is measured in **decibel (dB)**.

### Formula Relation

\[
\text{Loudness} \propto (\text{Amplitude})^2
\]

### Real-Life Example
If you hit a drum harder, the vibration amplitude increases, producing louder sound.

---

## Difference Between Pitch and Loudness

| Characteristic | Pitch | Loudness |
|---------------|-------|----------|
| Meaning | High or low sound | Loud or soft sound |
| Depends On | Frequency | Amplitude |
| Unit | Related to Hz | Decibel (dB) |
| Example | Flute vs Drum | Whisper vs Shout |

---

## Important Points

- Pitch does **not** depend on amplitude.
- Loudness does **not** depend on frequency directly.
- Two sounds may have same loudness but different pitch.
- Human ears can detect different pitch and loudness levels.

---

## Real-Life Observation

A child’s voice usually has higher pitch, while an adult male voice has lower pitch.  
A speaker volume increase changes loudness, not pitch.
