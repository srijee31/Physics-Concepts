# 25. Circular Motion

## 📌 What is Circular Motion?

Circular Motion is the motion of an object in a **circular path**.

👉 Even if speed is constant, direction keeps changing  
👉 So, the object is always accelerating

---

## 🧠 Key Concept

👉 Velocity changes because direction changes  
👉 Change in velocity = Acceleration  

This acceleration is called **Centripetal Acceleration**

---

## 📐 Centripetal Acceleration Formula

a = v² / r

Where:
- a = centripetal acceleration  
- v = velocity  
- r = radius of the circular path  

---

## 📐 Centripetal Force

To keep an object moving in a circle, a force is required towards the center.

F = m × v² / r

Where:
- F = centripetal force  
- m = mass  
- v = velocity  
- r = radius  

---

## 🔥 Real-Life Examples

- 🪨 Stone tied to a string and rotated  
- 🚗 Car taking a turn  
- 🌍 Planets revolving around the sun  

---

## ⚡ Key Points

- Direction of motion keeps changing  
- Velocity changes → acceleration exists  
- Force is always towards the center  
- Without centripetal force → object moves in straight line  

---

## 🧠 Example Problem

A car moves in a circle of radius 10 m with velocity 5 m/s.

a = v² / r  
a = 25 / 10  
a = 2.5 m/s²  

---

## 🧠 Summary

- Circular motion = motion in circular path  
- Centripetal acceleration = v² / r  
- Centripetal force = m v² / r  
- Direction constantly changes  

---

## ✅ One-Line Definition

👉 Circular motion is the motion of an object along a circular path with acceleration towards the center.
