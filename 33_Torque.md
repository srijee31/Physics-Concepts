# 33. Torque

## 📌 What is Torque?

Torque is the **turning effect of a force** acting on an object.

👉 It causes an object to rotate about an axis  

---

## 🧠 Simple Understanding

- Force makes object move  
- Torque makes object rotate  

👉 More torque = easier rotation  

---

## 📐 Formula

τ = r × F

Where:
- τ = torque  
- r = distance from axis (lever arm)  
- F = applied force  

---

## ⚡ Key Concept

👉 Torque depends on:
- Magnitude of force  
- Distance from pivot point  

👉 Greater distance → Greater torque  

---

## 🔥 Real-Life Examples

- 🔧 Using a spanner to open a nut  
- 🚪 Opening a door from the handle  
- 🚲 Pedaling a bicycle  

👉 Applying force farther from pivot = more effective  

---

## ⚡ Direction of Torque

- Clockwise or Anticlockwise  
- Determined by direction of force  

---

## ❗ Important Points

- If force is applied at pivot → torque = 0  
- Maximum torque when force is perpendicular  

---

## 🧠 Example Problem

A force of 10 N is applied at a distance of 2 m.

τ = 2 × 10  
τ = 20 Nm  

---

## 🧠 Summary

- Torque = turning effect of force  
- Formula: τ = r × F  
- Depends on force and distance  
- Measured in Newton-meter (Nm)  

---

## ✅ One-Line Definition

👉 Torque is the turning effect of a force about an axis.
