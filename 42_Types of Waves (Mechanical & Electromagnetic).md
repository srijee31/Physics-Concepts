# 42. Types of Waves (Mechanical & Electromagnetic)

## 🧠 Definition

Waves can be classified based on whether they require a medium for propagation or not.

Main types:
1. Mechanical Waves  
2. Electromagnetic Waves  

---

## 🎯 1. Mechanical Waves

### 📌 Definition
Mechanical waves are waves that **require a material medium** (solid, liquid, or gas) to travel.

### 🔄 Characteristics

- Cannot travel in vacuum  
- Transfer energy through particle vibrations  
- Speed depends on the medium  

### 🔥 Examples

- Sound waves  
- Water waves  
- Seismic waves  
- Waves on a rope  

---

## 🎯 2. Electromagnetic Waves

### 📌 Definition
Electromagnetic waves do **not require a material medium** and can travel through vacuum.

### 🔄 Characteristics

- Travel in vacuum  
- Consist of oscillating electric and magnetic fields  
- Travel at speed of light in vacuum  

### 🔥 Examples

- Radio waves  
- Microwaves  
- Infrared rays  
- Visible light  
- Ultraviolet rays  
- X-rays  
- Gamma rays  

---

## 🧮 Speed of Electromagnetic Waves in Vacuum

c = 3 × 10^8 m/s

Where:
- c = Speed of light  

---

## 📊 Difference Between Mechanical and Electromagnetic Waves

| Mechanical Waves | Electromagnetic Waves |
|------------------|----------------------|
| Need medium | No medium required |
| Cannot travel in vacuum | Can travel in vacuum |
| Produced by vibrations of particles | Produced by oscillating electric & magnetic fields |
| Example: Sound | Example: Light |

---

## ⚡ Key Points

- Mechanical waves need a medium  
- Electromagnetic waves can travel in space  
- Light is an electromagnetic wave  
- Sound is a mechanical wave
