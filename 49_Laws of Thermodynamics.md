# 49. Laws of Thermodynamics

## 📌 Definition
Thermodynamics is the branch of physics that deals with heat, work, temperature, and energy transformations.

---

## 🔥 Zeroth Law of Thermodynamics

### Statement:
If two systems are separately in thermal equilibrium with a third system, then they are in thermal equilibrium with each other.

### 🧠 Meaning:
- Defines the concept of **temperature**
- Helps in using thermometers

### 🌍 Example:
If A = C and B = C → then A = B (same temperature)

---

## ⚡ First Law of Thermodynamics (Law of Energy Conservation)

### Statement:
Energy can neither be created nor destroyed, only transformed from one form to another.

### 📊 Formula

ΔQ = ΔU + W

Where:
- ΔQ = Heat supplied  
- ΔU = Change in internal energy  
- W = Work done by the system  

---

## ❄️ Second Law of Thermodynamics

### Statement:
Heat cannot flow spontaneously from a colder body to a hotter body.

### 🧠 Meaning:
- Heat naturally flows **hot → cold**
- Introduces concept of **entropy (disorder)**

### 🌍 Examples:
- Ice melts in a hot room  
- Heat engine efficiency is never 100%  

---

## 🧊 Third Law of Thermodynamics

### Statement:
As temperature approaches absolute zero (0 K), the entropy of a perfect crystal approaches zero.

### 🧠 Meaning:
- Absolute zero cannot be practically achieved  
- System becomes perfectly ordered  

---

## 🎯 Key Points Summary

| Law        | Main Idea                          |
|------------|-----------------------------------|
| Zeroth     | Defines temperature               |
| First      | Energy conservation               |
| Second     | Direction of heat flow            |
| Third      | Behavior at absolute zero         |
