# 27. Gravitation

## 📌 What is Gravitation?

Gravitation is the **force of attraction between any two objects with mass**.

👉 Every object in the universe attracts every other object.

---

## 🌍 Special Case: Gravity

Gravity is a type of gravitation.

👉 It is the force with which the **Earth attracts objects towards its center**.

---

## 📐 Universal Law of Gravitation

Every two objects attract each other with a force:

F = (G × m₁ × m₂) / r²

Where:
- F = gravitational force  
- G = universal gravitational constant  
- m₁, m₂ = masses of objects  
- r = distance between their centers  

---

## ⚡ Key Points

- Force increases with mass  
- Force decreases with distance  
- If distance doubles → force becomes 1/4  
- Acts along the line joining two objects  

---

## 📉 Acceleration Due to Gravity (g)

g = 9.8 m/s² (on Earth)

👉 It is the acceleration with which objects fall towards Earth.

---

## 🔥 Real-Life Examples

- 🍎 Apple falling from a tree  
- 🌍 Planets revolving around the sun  
- 🌊 Tides in oceans (due to Moon’s gravity)  

---

## 🧠 Weight of an Object

Weight is the force with which Earth attracts an object.

W = m × g

Where:
- W = weight  
- m = mass  
- g = acceleration due to gravity  

---

## ⚡ Mass vs Weight

- Mass → constant everywhere  
- Weight → changes depending on gravity  

---

## 🧠 Example Problem

A body of mass 5 kg:

W = 5 × 9.8  
W = 49 N  

---

## 🧠 Summary

- Gravitation = attraction between masses  
- Formula: F = Gm₁m₂ / r²  
- Gravity is Earth’s attraction  
- g = 9.8 m/s²  
- Weight = m × g  

---

## ✅ One-Line Definition

👉 Gravitation is the force of attraction between any two objects having mass.
