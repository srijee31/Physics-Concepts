# 32. Rotational Motion

## 📌 What is Rotational Motion?

Rotational Motion is the motion of an object when it **rotates about a fixed axis**.

👉 Every point of the object moves in a circular path around the axis  

---

## 🧠 Examples

- 🌀 Fan rotating  
- 🚲 Wheel of a bike  
- 🌍 Earth rotating on its axis  

---

## 📐 Key Terms

### 1. Angular Displacement (θ)
- Angle through which object rotates  
- Measured in radians  

---

### 2. Angular Velocity (ω)

ω = θ / t  

Where:
- ω = angular velocity  
- θ = angular displacement  
- t = time  

---

### 3. Angular Acceleration (α)

α = (ω₂ − ω₁) / t  

👉 Rate of change of angular velocity  

---

## 🔁 Relation with Linear Motion

| Linear Motion | Rotational Motion |
|--------------|------------------|
| Displacement (s) | Angular displacement (θ) |
| Velocity (v) | Angular velocity (ω) |
| Acceleration (a) | Angular acceleration (α) |

---

## 📐 Important Relation

v = rω  

Where:
- v = linear velocity  
- r = radius  
- ω = angular velocity  

---

## ⚡ Key Points

- All points rotate around a fixed axis  
- Points farther from axis move faster  
- Direction continuously changes  

---

## 🔥 Real-Life Understanding

- Outer part of fan moves faster than inner part  
- Wheel rotates but center stays fixed  

---

## 🧠 Summary

- Rotational motion = motion about an axis  
- ω = θ / t  
- α = change in ω  
- v = rω  

---

## ✅ One-Line Definition

👉 Rotational motion is the motion of an object about a fixed axis.
