# 21. Law of Conservation of Momentum

## 📌 What is the Law of Conservation of Momentum?

The Law of Conservation of Momentum states that:

👉 **“Total momentum of a system remains constant if no external force acts on it.”**

---

## 🧠 Simple Meaning

- Momentum before interaction = Momentum after interaction  
- Applies to collisions and explosions  

---

## 📐 Formula

m₁u₁ + m₂u₂ = m₁v₁ + m₂v₂

Where:
- m₁, m₂ = masses  
- u₁, u₂ = initial velocities  
- v₁, v₂ = final velocities  

---

## 🔥 Real-Life Examples

- 🔫 Gun recoil  
  → Bullet moves forward, gun moves backward  

- 🚣 Person jumping from boat  
  → Boat moves in opposite direction  

- 🚗 Collision between two vehicles  

👉 Momentum is transferred but total remains same.

---

## ⚡ Key Points

- Works only if **no external force** acts  
- Applies to both **elastic and inelastic collisions**  
- Momentum is always conserved  

---

## 🔁 Why it Happens?

👉 When two objects interact:
- They apply equal and opposite forces  
- (Newton’s Third Law)

👉 So momentum lost by one = gained by other

---

## 🧠 Example Problem

Two objects:
- m₁ = 2 kg, u₁ = 3 m/s  
- m₂ = 1 kg, u₂ = 0 m/s  

After collision:
- v₁ = 1 m/s  
- v₂ = ?

Using formula:

(2×3) + (1×0) = (2×1) + (1×v₂)  
6 = 2 + v₂  
v₂ = 4 m/s  

---

## 🧠 Summary

- Total momentum before = Total momentum after  
- Formula: m₁u₁ + m₂u₂ = m₁v₁ + m₂v₂  
- Based on Newton’s Third Law  
- No external force → momentum conserved  

---

## ✅ One-Line Definition

👉 Total momentum of a system remains constant if no external force acts on it.
