# 39. Pendulum Motion

## 🧠 Definition
A simple pendulum consists of a **small mass (bob)** suspended from a fixed point by a **light, inextensible string**, which oscillates back and forth under the influence of gravity.

---

## 🎯 Condition for SHM

- When the angular displacement is **small (θ is small)**,  
  the motion of the pendulum approximates **Simple Harmonic Motion (SHM)**.

---

## 🧮 Time Period of a Simple Pendulum

T = 2π √(L / g)

Where:
- T = Time period  
- L = Length of the pendulum  
- g = Acceleration due to gravity  

---

## 📏 SI Units

- Time Period (T) → seconds (s)  
- Length (L) → meters (m)  
- g → m/s²  

---

## 🔄 Key Observations

- Time period **depends only on length (L) and gravity (g)**  
- It does **not depend on mass of the bob**  
- Longer pendulum → more time period  
- Stronger gravity → less time period  

---

## 🎯 Motion Characteristics

- Motion is **periodic and oscillatory**  
- Restoring force acts towards the **mean position**  
- Velocity is maximum at mean position  
- Velocity is zero at extreme positions  

---

## 🔥 Examples

- Swing in a playground  
- Wall clock pendulum  
- Hanging object oscillating  

---

## ⚡ Key Points

- Pendulum shows SHM for small angles  
- Time period formula: T = 2π √(L/g)  
- Independent of mass  
- Depends on length and gravity
