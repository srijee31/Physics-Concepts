# 35. Angular Velocity

## 🧠 Definition
Angular velocity is the **rate of change of angular displacement** with respect to time.

---

## 🧮 Formula

ω = θ / t

Where:
- ω (omega) = Angular velocity  
- θ (theta) = Angular displacement (in radians)  
- t = Time  

---

## 📏 SI Unit

- Radian per second (rad/s)

---

## 🔄 Relation with Linear Velocity

v = rω

Where:
- v = Linear velocity  
- r = Radius of circular path  
- ω = Angular velocity  

---

## 🎯 Direction of Angular Velocity

- Determined using the **Right-Hand Rule** ✋  
- Curl fingers in direction of rotation  
- Thumb points in direction of angular velocity  

---

## 🔥 Examples

- Rotation of a ceiling fan  
- Earth rotating on its axis  
- Wheel of a moving vehicle  

---

## ⚡ Key Points

- Angular velocity measures how fast an object rotates  
- It is a **vector quantity** (has both magnitude and direction)  
- Unit is rad/s  
- Related to linear velocity using: v = rω
