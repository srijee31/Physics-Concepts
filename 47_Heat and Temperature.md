# 47. Heat and Temperature

## 📌 Definition

### 🔥 Heat
Heat is a form of energy that transfers from a hotter body to a colder body due to a temperature difference.

### 🌡️ Temperature
Temperature is the measure of the degree of hotness or coldness of a body.

---

## 🎯 Key Difference

| Feature        | Heat 🔥                          | Temperature 🌡️                     |
|----------------|----------------------------------|----------------------------------|
| Meaning        | Energy transfer                  | Measure of hotness/coldness      |
| Depends on     | Mass + material + temp difference| Only on average kinetic energy   |
| Unit           | Joule (J), Calorie (cal)         | Kelvin (K), Celsius (°C)         |
| Nature         | Extensive property               | Intensive property               |
| Flow           | Flows from hot → cold            | Does not flow                    |

---

## ⚡ Important Concept

- Heat flows **only when there is temperature difference**
- No temperature difference = No heat transfer

---

## 🧠 Microscopic View

- Temperature → Average kinetic energy of particles  
- Heat → Total energy transferred between particles  

---

## 📊 Formula

Q = mcΔT

Where:
- Q = Heat energy  
- m = Mass of substance  
- c = Specific heat capacity  
- ΔT = Change in temperature  

---

## 🌡️ Units

- Heat → Joule (J)
- Temperature → Kelvin (SI unit)

---

## 🌍 Real-Life Examples

- Heating water on a stove  
- Ice melting into water  
- Hot tea cooling down  
- Metal spoon getting hot in tea  

---

## 💡 Quick Memory Trick

"Temperature tells how hot, Heat tells how much energy flows"
