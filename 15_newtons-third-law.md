# Newton’s Third Law of Motion

## 1. What is it?
Newton’s Third Law of Motion states that for every action, there is an equal and opposite reaction. This means that forces always exist in pairs.

## 2. Simple Meaning
Whenever object A pushes object B, object B pushes back on object A with the exact same amount of force, but in the opposite direction.



## 3. The Concept
If you exert a force ($F_{ab}$) on an object, that object exerts a force ($F_{ba}$) back on you:
$$F_{ab} = -F_{ba}$$
*(The negative sign indicates the opposite direction)*

## 4. Real-Life Examples
* **Walking:** When you walk, your foot pushes the ground backward (Action). The ground pushes your foot forward (Reaction), which allows you to move.
* **Rocket Launch:** A rocket pushes burning gases downward out of its engine (Action). The gases push the rocket upward (Reaction).
* **Swimming:** A swimmer pushes the water backward with their hands (Action). The water pushes the swimmer forward (Reaction).



## 5. Why is it used?
* **Aerodynamics:** To design airplanes and drones that can lift off the ground.
* **Sports Science:** To understand how athletes can jump higher by pushing harder against the ground.
* **Space Exploration:** To navigate satellites and spacecraft in the vacuum of space using thrusters.

## 6. Key Points
* **Action and Reaction:** These forces always happen at the exact same time.
* **Two Different Objects:** Action and reaction forces act on different objects, so they do not cancel each other out.
* **Equality:** The strength of the "push" and the "pull" is always exactly equal.

### 📝 Think About It
If you are standing on a skateboard and you throw a heavy ball forward, why do you move backward?
* **Answer:** Because as you push the ball forward (Action), the ball pushes you backward with an equal force (Reaction)!
