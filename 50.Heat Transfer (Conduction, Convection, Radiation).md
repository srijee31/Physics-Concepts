# 50. Heat Transfer (Conduction, Convection, Radiation)

## 📌 Definition
Heat transfer is the process by which heat energy moves from a region of higher temperature to a region of lower temperature.

---

## 🔥 Modes of Heat Transfer

### 1️⃣ Conduction

#### 📌 Definition:
Transfer of heat through a material without movement of the material itself.

#### 🧠 Mechanism:
- Heat is transferred via particle vibrations and collisions

#### 🌍 Examples:
- Metal spoon getting hot in tea  
- Iron rod heated at one end  

#### ⚡ Key Points:
- Occurs mainly in solids  
- No actual movement of particles  

---

### 2️⃣ Convection

#### 📌 Definition:
Transfer of heat through the movement of fluids (liquids or gases).

#### 🧠 Mechanism:
- Hot fluid becomes less dense and rises  
- Cold fluid becomes denser and sinks  
- Creates convection currents  

#### 🌍 Examples:
- Boiling water  
- Sea breeze and land breeze  
- Air circulation in a room  

#### ⚡ Key Points:
- Occurs in liquids and gases  
- Involves bulk movement of particles  

---

### 3️⃣ Radiation

#### 📌 Definition:
Transfer of heat in the form of electromagnetic waves without any medium.

#### 🧠 Mechanism:
- Heat travels as infrared radiation  

#### 🌍 Examples:
- Heat from the Sun reaching Earth  
- Feeling heat from a fire without touching it  

#### ⚡ Key Points:
- Does not require a medium  
- Travels in vacuum  

---

## 📊 Comparison Table

| Mode        | Medium Required | Particle Movement | Example                  |
|-------------|----------------|-------------------|--------------------------|
| Conduction  | Yes            | No                | Hot metal rod            |
| Convection  | Yes            | Yes               | Boiling water            |
| Radiation   | No             | No                | Sun heating Earth        |

---

## 🌍 Real-Life Applications

- Cooking utensils (conduction)  
- Room heaters and AC airflow (convection)  
- Solar energy (radiation)
