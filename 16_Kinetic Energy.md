# 16. Kinetic Energy

## What is it?

Kinetic Energy is the energy possessed by an object due to its **motion**. Any object that is moving has kinetic energy.

---

## Simple Meaning

👉 If it moves, it has Kinetic Energy. The faster it moves or the heavier it is, the more energy it has.

---

## Formula

The kinetic energy ($K$) of an object is calculated using:

$$K = \frac{1}{2}mv^2$$

- **$m$**: Mass of the object (kg)
- **$v$**: Velocity or speed (m/s)



---

## Example

- A cricket ball flying through the air
- A car driving on a highway
- Water flowing in a river
- A person running a race

---

## Why it is used?

- To calculate the impact force in collisions (like car safety tests)
- To understand how much work a moving object can do
- To design wind turbines and hydroelectric power plants

---

## Key Points

- **Always Positive:** Kinetic energy cannot be negative.
- **Speed Matters:** If you double the speed, the kinetic energy becomes **four times** greater ($2^2 = 4$).
- **Mass Matters:** A heavy truck has more kinetic energy than a small car moving at the same speed.
- **Unit:** It is measured in **Joules (J)**.
