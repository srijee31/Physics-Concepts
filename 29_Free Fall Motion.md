# 29. Free Fall Motion

## 📌 What is Free Fall?

Free fall is the motion of an object when it falls **only under the influence of gravity**.

👉 No other forces (like air resistance) are considered  

---

## 🧠 Key Concept

- Only force acting = gravitational force  
- Acceleration = g (9.8 m/s²)  
- Direction = downward  

---

## 📐 Equations of Motion (for Free Fall)

v = u + gt  
s = ut + (1/2)gt²  
v² = u² + 2gs  

Where:
- u = initial velocity  
- v = final velocity  
- g = 9.8 m/s²  
- t = time  
- s = displacement  

---

## ⚡ Special Case (Object Dropped)

If an object is dropped:

- u = 0  

So equations become:

v = gt  
s = (1/2)gt²  
v² = 2gs  

---

## 🔥 Real-Life Examples

- 🍎 Apple falling from tree  
- 🪨 Stone dropped from height  
- 🏢 Object falling from building  

---

## ⚡ Key Points

- All objects fall at same rate (in vacuum)  
- Mass does not affect free fall  
- Velocity increases every second  
- Acceleration is constant  

---

## 🧠 Example Problem

A stone is dropped from rest for 3 seconds.

u = 0, g = 9.8 m/s²  

v = 9.8 × 3  
v = 29.4 m/s  

s = (1/2) × 9.8 × (3²)  
s = 4.9 × 9  
s = 44.1 m  

---

## 🧠 Summary

- Free fall = motion under gravity only  
- Acceleration = g  
- Use standard motion equations with g  
- Independent of mass  

---

## ✅ One-Line Definition

👉 Free fall is the motion of an object under the influence of gravity alone.
