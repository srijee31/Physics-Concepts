# 20. Momentum

## 📌 What is Momentum?
Momentum is the **quantity of motion** of an object.

👉 It depends on:
- Mass of the object  
- Velocity of the object  

---

## 📐 Formula of Momentum

p = m × v

Where:
- p = momentum  
- m = mass (in kg)  
- v = velocity (in m/s)

---

## 🔥 Real-Life Examples

- 🚚 Truck moving → High momentum (large mass)  
- 🏃 Person running → Moderate momentum  
- ⚽ Ball rolling → Low momentum  

👉 Heavier and faster objects have more momentum.

---

## ⚡ Key Points

- More mass → More momentum  
- More velocity → More momentum  
- If velocity = 0 → Momentum = 0  

---

## 💥 Momentum vs Kinetic Energy

- Momentum ∝ velocity  
- Kinetic Energy ∝ velocity²  

👉 Small increase in speed → KE increases faster than momentum

---

## 🔁 Change in Momentum

Change in momentum happens when:
- Speed changes  
- Direction changes  

👉 This change is caused by **force**

---

## 🧠 Example Problem

A 5 kg object is moving with velocity 2 m/s.

p = 5 × 2  
p = 10 kg·m/s

---

## 🧠 Summary

- Momentum = mass × velocity  
- Formula: p = mv  
- Measured in kg·m/s  
- Depends on mass and velocity  

---

## ✅ One-Line Definition

👉 Momentum is the quantity of motion possessed by an object.
