# 31. Work-Energy Theorem

## 📌 What is Work-Energy Theorem?

The Work-Energy Theorem states:

👉 **“Work done on an object is equal to the change in its kinetic energy.”**

---

## 🧠 Simple Meaning

- When work is done on an object → its speed changes  
- Change in speed → change in kinetic energy  

👉 So,
Work done = Change in Kinetic Energy  

---

## 📐 Formula

W = ΔKE  

W = (1/2)mv² − (1/2)mu²  

Where:
- W = work done  
- m = mass  
- u = initial velocity  
- v = final velocity  

---

## ⚡ Key Understanding

- If work is positive → kinetic energy increases  
- If work is negative → kinetic energy decreases  

---

## 🔥 Real-Life Examples

- 🚗 Car accelerating → work increases KE  
- 🛑 Braking → work decreases KE  
- ⚽ Kicking a ball → increases KE  

---

## ⚡ Important Points

- Work causes change in motion  
- Energy is transferred through work  
- Connects force and energy concepts  

---

## 🧠 Example Problem

A 2 kg object increases velocity from 2 m/s to 6 m/s.

W = (1/2) × 2 × (6² − 2²)  
W = 1 × (36 − 4)  
W = 32 Joules  

---

## 🧠 Summary

- Work done = change in kinetic energy  
- Formula: W = ΔKE  
- Depends on mass and velocity  
- Explains how motion changes  

---

## ✅ One-Line Definition

👉 Work done on an object is equal to the change in its kinetic energy.
