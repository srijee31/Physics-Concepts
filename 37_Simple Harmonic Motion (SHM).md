# 37. Simple Harmonic Motion (SHM)

## 🧠 Definition
Simple Harmonic Motion (SHM) is a type of oscillatory motion in which the **restoring force is directly proportional to the displacement** from the mean position and always acts towards it.

---

## 🧮 Equation of SHM

F = -kx

Where:
- F = Restoring force  
- k = Force constant  
- x = Displacement from mean position  
- Negative sign indicates force is opposite to displacement  

---

## 📌 Displacement Equation

x = A sin(ωt + φ)

Where:
- x = Displacement  
- A = Amplitude (maximum displacement)  
- ω = Angular frequency  
- t = Time  
- φ = Phase constant  

---

## 📏 Time Period

T = 2π / ω

---

## 🔁 Frequency

f = 1 / T

---

## ⚡ Angular Frequency

ω = 2πf

---

## 🎯 Important Terms

- **Mean Position** → Central position where net force is zero  
- **Amplitude (A)** → Maximum displacement  
- **Time Period (T)** → Time for one complete oscillation  
- **Frequency (f)** → Number of oscillations per second  

---

## 🔥 Examples

- Oscillation of a spring  
- Simple pendulum (small angle)  
- Vibrations of a tuning fork  

---

## ⚡ Key Points

- SHM is a **periodic motion**  
- Restoring force always acts towards mean position  
- Motion follows a **sine/cosine pattern**  
- Energy continuously converts between **kinetic and potential energy**
