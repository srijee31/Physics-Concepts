# 36. Angular Acceleration

## 🧠 Definition
Angular acceleration is the **rate of change of angular velocity** with respect to time.

---

## 🧮 Formula

α = (ω₂ - ω₁) / t

Where:
- α (alpha) = Angular acceleration  
- ω₁ = Initial angular velocity  
- ω₂ = Final angular velocity  
- t = Time  

---

## 📏 SI Unit

- Radian per second squared (rad/s²)

---

## 🔄 Relation with Linear Acceleration

a = rα

Where:
- a = Linear acceleration  
- r = Radius of circular path  
- α = Angular acceleration  

---

## 🎯 Types of Angular Acceleration

1. **Uniform Angular Acceleration**
   - Angular velocity changes at a constant rate  

2. **Non-uniform Angular Acceleration**
   - Angular velocity changes at a varying rate  

---

## 🔥 Examples

- A fan speeding up when switched on  
- A bike wheel slowing down when brakes are applied  
- Rotating disc increasing its speed  

---

## ⚡ Key Points

- Angular acceleration measures how quickly angular velocity changes  
- It is a **vector quantity**  
- Unit is rad/s²  
- Related to linear acceleration using: a = rα
